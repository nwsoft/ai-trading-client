# arXiv Submission Checklist

This checklist captures the recommended preparation steps for the NoahAI paper before arXiv submission.

## Manuscript Requirements

- Use the English manuscript as the primary submission file.
- Make sure the title matches the archive version.
- Keep the author line concise and venue-friendly.
- Remove internal provenance statements that are not appropriate for the main arXiv text.
- Keep the abstract short, direct, and technical.

## File Packaging

- Prepare the final PDF.
- Prepare the source bundle required by arXiv.
- Ensure figures are self-contained and correctly referenced.
- Verify that the bibliography compiles cleanly.

## Metadata

- Title: NoahAI-DAL: A Governable Financial Decision Architecture with XAI and Risk Governance
- Author: JUNG HAESUNG
- Affiliation: Dream AI Lab, NoahAI Labs
- Category: choose the most appropriate arXiv subject area for finance, AI, or machine learning
- Comments: mention the Zenodo DOI after the archive record is published if appropriate

## Review Before Upload

- Check spelling, section numbering, and citation consistency.
- Confirm that the English manuscript matches the archived version.
- Verify that no internal note leaks into the public submission file.
- Keep the Korean manuscript in the Zenodo archive, not as the arXiv main submission file.
